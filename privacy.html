Premier Digital Program — Privacy Policy, Version 35.2

Introduction

MISBAHUL 'ILM SCHOOLS ABUJA respects the privacy of parents, learners, staff members, visitors and other individuals who interact with the Premier Digital Program platform.

This Privacy Policy describes the general principles under which information provided through this website and its related services may be handled.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the service being used, the platform may receive information voluntarily provided by a visitor, parent, learner, staff member or authorized administrator.

Examples may include:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone number.
  • Information submitted through forms.
  • Program-related information.
  • Information required to respond to enquiries.

How Information May Be Used

Information supplied through the platform may be used to provide requested services, respond to enquiries, communicate with users and improve the digital experience.

  • Responding to enquiries and requests.
  • Providing information about the program.
  • Supporting registration processes.
  • Improving website functionality.
  • Maintaining platform security.

Children's Information

The Premier Digital Program may provide services intended for children as well as adults. Where information concerning a child is required, parents, guardians or authorized school personnel should provide and manage such information responsibly.

Parent & Guardian Awareness

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of digital services and to contact the school whenever they have questions concerning information submitted through the program.

Information Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures may be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

Users should nevertheless understand that no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely immune from security risks.

Third-Party Services

Certain website functions may depend on external technology providers or services. Where such services are used, information may be processed according to the relevant service provider's policies and technical requirements.

Cookies & Technical Information

The website or supporting services may use technical information such as browser, device or session information to maintain functionality, security and performance.

Where cookies or similar technologies are used, they may assist with improving navigation and the overall user experience.

Your Choices

Users may contact the school when they have questions about information submitted through the platform or wish to request clarification regarding its handling.

Requests may be reviewed according to the applicable school procedures and legal requirements.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the Premier Digital Program develops, website functionality changes or applicable requirements are updated.

The version displayed on this page indicates the current website version associated with this policy.
