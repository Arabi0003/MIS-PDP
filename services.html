Special ICT Programme

Digital skills for today's world.

A carefully structured digital-skills programme designed to introduce children and adults to practical information and communication technology, responsible digital participation and confidence with modern technology.

What We Offer

Practical technology learning with purpose.

Our Special ICT programme is presented around practical digital skills, guided learning and responsible technology use. The programme is suitable for learners at different stages of their digital journey.

SERVICE 01
💻

Computer Fundamentals

Introduction to computers, hardware, software, operating systems, file management and everyday computer use.

SERVICE 02
🌐

Internet Skills

Learn how to navigate the internet, communicate online, find information and use digital services responsibly.

SERVICE 03
📱

Digital Device Skills

Practical guidance for using computers, smartphones, tablets and other everyday digital devices.

SERVICE 04
📝

Digital Productivity

Build confidence with documents, presentations, spreadsheets and other productivity tools.

SERVICE 05
🎨

Creative Technology

Encourage learners to use technology creatively for communication, projects, presentations and digital expression.

SERVICE 06
🛡️

Digital Responsibility

Develop awareness of responsible, respectful and safe participation in the digital environment.

The Learning Approach

Technology should be understandable.

We focus on making digital learning clear, practical and approachable. Learners can develop skills progressively rather than simply memorising technical terminology.

Practical Learning Emphasis on useful skills that can be applied beyond the classroom.
Progressive Development Skills can be introduced progressively according to learner ability.
Responsible Technology Use Digital awareness forms an important part of modern ICT participation.
Learner Confidence The goal is to help learners become more comfortable using technology.
Who Can Benefit

Designed for different stages of learning.

The programme can serve learners who are discovering technology for the first time as well as people seeking to strengthen their existing digital confidence.

For Children

Help young learners develop a healthy, practical foundation for using technology as part of modern life.

  • Computer awareness
  • Basic digital skills
  • Internet awareness
  • Creative technology activities
  • Responsible digital behaviour

For Adults

Build confidence with everyday technology and digital tools that can support personal, educational and professional activities.

  • Computer fundamentals
  • Internet and online services
  • Digital productivity
  • Device confidence
  • Practical digital skills
Parents & Guardians

A programme parents can understand.

Digital technology is becoming part of education, communication and everyday life. Parents and guardians can use this programme as an opportunity to encourage constructive technology awareness and practical digital development.

Encourage useful technology Help learners understand that technology can be used for learning, creativity and productive activities.
Build confidence gradually Learners can develop their understanding step by step.
Promote responsible use Digital awareness should accompany technical ability.
Stay involved Parents and guardians remain important partners in a learner's development.
